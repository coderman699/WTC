About Us

Our Story

Acme Co. began in 2015 as a small workshop with one simple idea: make things people will actually keep.

Over a decade later, we have grown into a team of designers, makers, and customer advocates.

Our Mission

To craft everyday goods that earn their place in your life — through quality materials and honest design.

Our Values

  1. Craft — We design slowly and build carefully.
  2. Honesty — We tell you what something is made of and where it came from.
  3. Longevity — Products should outlive trends.
  4. People — We answer every email ourselves.
